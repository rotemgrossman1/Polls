Create poll: components

Direction: Option B, Playful. Spec: specs/features/2026-09-14-create-poll.md. Frames are 360px mobile unless marked desktop. The desktop frames cross the md breakpoint: the bottom bar stops sticking and the sheet becomes a centered dialog.

1 · Landing2 · Form3 · Options: drag and limits4 · Errors and saving5 · Discard dialog6 · Confirmation7 · Desktop

1 · Landing page

HeroCard uses the emphasis-card pattern (tint + ink outline) with the tilted bars mark. The primary Button sits outside the card, so there is one orange focal point.

2 · Create poll form

The question lives in the tinted emphasis card. AnswerTypeSelector reuses the answer-tile pattern (selected = filled action + check). OptionEditorRow is a tile: the header holds drag handle, number, move and remove buttons, and the input spans the full width below so long text wraps. Counters sit at the right of each field's helper row.

3 · Options: drag, limits, counters

While dragging, the row lifts (--shadow-md plus a --rotate-tilt-sm tilt, --ease-emphasized on pick-up) and a dashed slot shows where it will land. At 8 options, "Add option" becomes aria-disabled and the limit hint appears with an icon. A counter at its limit turns ink and bold.

4 · Validation, saving, save failure

Field errors follow the brief: icon + bold danger text + danger border and tint, linked with aria-describedby. An invalid option row also turns its tile border danger. While saving, the primary button shows a spinner with the spec's "Creating…" label, and every control is locked. A failed save shows one Alert above the bottom bar.

5 · ConfirmDialog: discard [Could]

A bottom sheet on mobile. The danger action sits on top and the safe action at the bottom, near the thumb. Initial focus goes to "Keep editing", and Escape counts as Keep editing. From md it becomes a centered dialog (see Desktop).

6 · Confirmation screen

A SuccessMark (check icon in a success-tinted circle) gives a playful success moment. PollSummary is read-only: the option rows are plain numbered list items with dividers, with no tile outline or shadow, so they don't look tappable. "Back to home" is primary for now. When Share invite link ships, its action becomes primary and both of these become secondary.

7 · Desktop (md and up)

Same single column, capped at --container-max. The bottom bar is no longer sticky: Cancel and Create poll sit side by side, right-aligned. The sheet becomes a centered dialog with its safe action on the left.
