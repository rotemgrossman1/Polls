1 · Register & log in

Pattern A: one screen, a segmented control switches between Log in and Register. Field errors sit under the field (icon + text + thicker border).

2 · Create poll

Pattern A: a plain form. Question, then a list of option inputs, each with a remove button, and a dashed “Add option” button. On mobile the submit button is pinned to the bottom so it is always reachable.

3 · Share the invite link

Pattern A: after creating, the user lands on a full “poll ready” page (no modal). Link field + Copy button, plus a Share button that opens the phone's native share sheet. Copy success is a Toast.

4 · Accept invite + enter nickname (guest)

Pattern A: the guest sees a PollCard preview of what they were invited to, then one nickname field. Invalid link = EmptyState. Closed poll = the same card with a Closed badge and a results button.

5 · Pick an answer

Pattern A: classic radio cards. The whole row is tappable, selected = filled radio dot + blue border + tint (shape cue, not color alone). Try it: click or use arrow keys.

6 · Results

Pattern A: one list of horizontal bars in poll order. Label above the bar on mobile, beside it on desktop. Leader = darker bar + “Leading” tag with a star. The guest's own choice = “Your vote” tag with a check.
